01 / The content collection

Content strategy

A clear direction for every post.

Every strong feed starts with a purpose. This section brings together content pillars, audience insights and ideas that give each post a clear role.

Read moreRead less

Start with a purpose.

Content strategy connects business goals with the interests of your audience. It defines who you want to reach, what you want to say and which topics deserve a regular place in your feed. Clear content pillars help turn individual ideas into a consistent direction.

The process can include audience research, a review of existing content, tone-of-voice guidelines and a mix of educational, inspirational and promotional formats. Each piece should have a clear purpose and a useful next step for the reader.

02 / The content collection

Visual identity

A recognisable look across your feed.

Colour, typography and composition create a familiar visual language. Explore a consistent approach to content that feels connected across different formats.

Read moreRead less

Make every touchpoint feel connected.

Visual identity brings colour, typography, imagery and layout into one recognisable system. Consistent design helps a brand feel familiar, whether someone sees a single post, a story or an entire campaign.

A flexible set of templates keeps the overall look coherent while leaving room for different messages. Spacing, contrast and clear visual hierarchy make the content easier to read on small screens.

03 / The content collection

Social posts

Small formats. Strong impressions.

A single post can tell a complete story. Here, clear layouts and focused messages make space for announcements, products and everyday inspiration.

Read moreRead less

One post. One clear message.

A strong social post gives the viewer a clear point of focus. The image, headline and caption should work together to introduce a product, share an idea or start a conversation without competing for attention.

The design starts with the message and the intended audience. From there, the composition, image crop and call to action are adapted to the format, keeping the result clear and easy to recognise in a busy feed.

04 / The content collection

Carousels

Stories worth swiping through.

One idea unfolds across a sequence of slides. Carousels turn useful information into small, easy-to-follow stories that invite the next swipe.

Read moreRead less

Build a story one slide at a time.

A carousel gives an idea room to unfold. The first slide introduces a reason to keep reading, the middle slides develop the story and the final slide offers a useful takeaway or a clear next step.

A consistent visual rhythm helps connect the sequence. Short text, purposeful pacing and one main idea per slide make carousels useful for product introductions, practical guides and step-by-step explanations.

05 / The content collection

Stories

Everyday moments, thoughtfully framed.

Stories create room for a more spontaneous voice. Short updates, questions and behind-the-scenes moments keep the conversation moving.

Read moreRead less

Create room for everyday interaction.

Stories offer a more immediate way to communicate. They can introduce a new product, show a detail behind the scenes or invite the audience to respond to a question. A short sequence can turn a simple update into a small narrative.

Vertical layouts need readable type and generous space around key elements. The design should also leave room for platform controls and interactive features such as polls, questions or links.

06 / The content collection

Reels & video

Ideas that come to life in motion.

Movement adds a new layer to an idea. This space is for short video concepts, simple storyboards and visual rhythms designed for the small screen.

Read moreRead less

Give the idea a rhythm.

Short video combines movement, imagery, text and sound to communicate an idea over time. A clear opening helps establish the subject, while considered pacing keeps the sequence easy to follow.

Planning starts with a simple concept and storyboard. Product details, transitions and captions should support the message. On-screen text can also make the story understandable when the viewer watches without sound.

Find the words that belong to the brand.

Copywriting turns a message into language people can understand and connect with. Headlines create interest, captions add context and calls to action make the next step clear. The right tone should feel consistent with the brand and appropriate for its audience.

Good copy starts with a specific idea. It puts the most useful information first, avoids unnecessary wording and gives each sentence a purpose. The same message can then be adapted for a post, a story or a campaign.

08 / The content collection

Campaigns

One idea. A connected collection.

A campaign connects individual pieces through one central idea. Explore how a visual direction can stretch across posts, stories and supporting formats.

Read moreRead less

Connect every format to one idea.

A campaign brings several pieces of content together around a shared goal. A central message and visual direction help posts, stories, carousels and videos feel like parts of the same story.

Planning considers how the audience will encounter the content over time: an introduction, a closer look and a clear invitation to act. Each format contributes something useful while keeping the overall identity consistent.

09 / The content collection

Content planning

A consistent rhythm for your content.

A thoughtful schedule gives good ideas room to grow. Content calendars bring topics, formats and publishing moments into a clear, manageable rhythm.

Read moreRead less

Turn ideas into a practical schedule.

Content planning organises topics, formats and publishing dates into a manageable calendar. It helps balance recurring themes with timely updates and makes it easier to prepare the images and copy each piece needs.

A useful plan includes the purpose, format and status of every item, alongside space for review. Regularly looking back at audience responses can help refine the next set of ideas and keep the schedule relevant.
